Our Story

Born in
People's Colony
for the People

Tucked in the heart of People's Colony, Gujranwala, Yumilicious was built on one simple belief — great food should make you feel something. Every slice, every bite, every crunch.

From our signature Malai Boti Pizza dripping with creamy cheese to our perfectly seasoned Loaded Fries — we craft each dish with premium ingredients and passion.

Walk in, order in, or get it delivered. The experience stays the same: unforgettably delicious.
